Caveats from the harness:

- UNATTRIBUTED means the rendering matches none of the approved translations in this index. It is not a claim that the text is wrong, and a narrow index will produce more of them.
- These numbers describe MIZAN's behaviour on this corpus. They say nothing about how often publishers misquote; that was not measured and this corpus could not support such a claim.
- Ground truth for reference accuracy is the publisher's own printed citation, not an independent adjudication.
- The corpus is drawn from a small number of hosts and is not a random sample of da'wah publishing.
